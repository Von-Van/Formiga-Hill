Formiga Hill

Drag Formiga Hill into Applications, and Formiga Desktop will find it there on its own. To send
the colony on a visit, choose "Go to Formiga Hill…" from Formiga's menu and they will take the
train.

The download is not signed yet, so the first time you open it, Control-click the app and choose
Open.

Formiga Hill never changes your colony. Whatever happens at the Hill, everyone comes home exactly
as they left.
